QUAKER - PLAYER AID (the Quick reference, last page of the rulebook)
One shared copy: keep it open where all players can see it.

EVERY ROUND
1. Event - the first player flips and reads the top event card.
2. Draw - everyone draws 1 block card.
3. Build - clockwise from the first player, place the block on your card.
4. Shake - the first player shakes the tower base for 5 seconds.
5. Check - has the tower collapsed? If not, discard the cards, pass the first player clockwise and start a new round.

BEFORE ROUND 1 OF EVERY GAME
Opening placement: clockwise from the first player, each player places any 1 block from the supply, no card needed.
In the first game, the first name entered at setup is the first player.

PLACING
Only the style on your card - on the spaces or on top of blocks - any angle - nothing hanging off the base -
you may slide under an overhang - once you let go, it stays.

WHEN THE TOWER FALLS
It has collapsed once half of its tallest (at least 2 blocks) has come down, counted over the whole game.
The player who placed the most recent block loses. Everyone else scores 1 point.
First to 3 points wins the match. Players keep count themselves.

BLOCK DECK TOO SHORT?
Nobody draws or places. Play Event, Shake and Check only, and shake a little harder each round until the tower collapses.
